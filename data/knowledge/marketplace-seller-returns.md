# Scope
Items sold by third-party marketplace sellers are marked "Sold by" a seller other than Brightcart on the product
page. This article applies only to those items. Items sold by Brightcart follow the standard returns policy.

# Return window
Marketplace items can be returned within 14 days of delivery for a change of mind. Each seller may charge its own
restocking fee of up to 20 percent.

# Damaged marketplace items
Report damage to the seller first through the order page. If the seller does not respond within 2 business days,
Brightcart support can step in under the A-to-Z guarantee and refund up to USD 1,000.
