title: Returns and Refund Policy
doc_type: policy
---
# Change of mind
Undamaged items may be returned for any reason within 30 days of the delivery date. The refund is the item price
minus a 15 percent restocking fee. The customer pays return shipping unless their loyalty tier says otherwise.
After 30 days, change-of-mind returns are not accepted and no refund or credit is offered.

# Damaged or wrong items
Damaged or wrong items must be reported within 7 days of delivery. The customer may choose a full refund of the
item price or a replacement of the same item. No restocking fee applies. Damaged items with a retail price below
USD 100 do not need to be sent back. Items of USD 100 or more must be returned with a prepaid return label, which
costs the store USD 6.

# Shipping fees on refunds
Standard shipping fees are refunded only when every item in the order is refunded or the order was not received.
Express shipping fees are covered by the shipping policy.

# Store credit
Instead of a cash refund, the customer may accept store credit worth 110 percent of the refund amount. Offer it,
but never force it.

# Refund method
Refunds go to the original payment method. Never send a refund to a different card, account or person.
