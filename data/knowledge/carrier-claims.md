# When to open a claim
Open a carrier claim when a parcel is lost, when tracking shows delivered but the customer did not receive it, or
when a parcel arrives visibly crushed.

# How
Use the carrier portal with the tracking number, the delivery address and the declared value. Claims are decided by
the carrier in 5 to 10 business days. The outcome of a claim does not delay the customer's refund once it is
approved under Brightcart policy.

# Recoveries
Money recovered from the carrier goes to Brightcart, not to the customer.
