# Destinations and fees
Brightcart ships to the United States and Canada.
- United States standard: USD 5.99, promised within 5 business days.
- United States express: USD 14.99 to USD 19.99 depending on weight, promised within 2 business days.
- Canada: flat USD 14.99, delivery in 7 to 10 business days. Duties are paid by the customer.
Brightcart does not ship to other countries.

# Late delivery credit
An order is late when it is delivered after its promised date. Days late are the calendar days between the
promised date and the delivery date. The customer receives a late-delivery credit of 5 percent of the order
subtotal for each day late, capped at 20 percent of the subtotal. The subtotal is the item prices only, without
shipping. If the order used express shipping and was late, the express fee is also refunded in full.
The credit is paid as a refund to the original payment method.

# Not received
If tracking shows the parcel as not delivered, refund the full order including shipping, or reship it if the
customer prefers. If tracking shows the parcel as delivered but the customer says it never arrived, open a
carrier claim and follow the fraud and abuse policy before refunding.
