# Coverage
Electronics and small appliances carry a 1-year warranty from the delivery date against manufacturing defects.
Kitchenware and accessories carry a 90-day warranty.

# Remedy
Within warranty, a defective item is replaced with the same item if it is in stock, otherwise repaired. Warranty
claims are never settled as cash refunds once the 30-day return window has passed.

# Exclusions
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, drops, liquid damage, normal wear, or cosmetic changes such as
colour fading. Dislike of an item, including its colour or size, is not a defect.
