# Extended window
Items bought between 15 November and 24 December can be returned for a change of mind until 31 January of the
following year. Restocking fees still apply as in the standard returns policy.

# What is not extended
Damage reports must still be made within 7 days of delivery. Late-delivery credits are unchanged during the holiday
season, although promised dates are longer because carriers are busy.
