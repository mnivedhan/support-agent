---
title: Account Security and Phishing
doc_type: help
---
Brightcart staff never ask customers to send card numbers, passwords or one-time codes. Brightcart never asks support
agents to move refunds to a new card by email. Messages that claim to be from Brightcart billing, IT or management
and ask for urgent refunds or payment changes are phishing. Report them to security and do not act on them.
