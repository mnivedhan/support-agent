# About the card
The Brightcart Rewards Visa is issued by a partner bank, not by Brightcart. It has its own tiers: Blue, Gold and
Platinum, based on card spend. These card tiers are separate from Brightcart loyalty tiers.

# Card Gold benefits
Card Gold members earn 3 percent back on Brightcart purchases and get extended purchase protection of 90 days
against accidental damage, claimed through the bank. Card benefits never change Brightcart refund amounts,
late-delivery credits or approval rules.

# Disputes
Card disputes and chargebacks are handled by the bank. Support agents cannot see card account details.
