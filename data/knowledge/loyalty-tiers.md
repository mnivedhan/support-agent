# Tiers
Customers are Standard, Silver or Gold based on spend in the last 12 months.

# Standard
No extra benefits.

# Silver (USD 500 or more per year)
Free return shipping on change-of-mind returns.

# Gold (USD 1,500 or more per year)
- Free return shipping on change-of-mind returns.
- Late delivery credit is 10 percent of the order subtotal per day late, capped at 30 percent of the subtotal,
  instead of the standard 5 percent per day capped at 20 percent.
- Priority reply within 4 hours.
Loyalty tier does not change approval thresholds, return windows or fraud checks.
